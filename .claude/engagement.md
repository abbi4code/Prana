# Engagement: Weekly Wrapped, festival days, calorie bank, fasting

Roadmap v2 (features.md). Order agreed with the owner 2026-09-25: **Wrapped → festival mode → calorie bank → fasting**; barcode + photo logging are in [smart-logging.md](smart-logging.md).

## Owner decisions (2026-09-25)
| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Order | Weekly Wrapped first (no new rules), then festival mode, calorie bank, fasting |
| Calorie bank | **Plan ahead**: pick a splurge day in advance (e.g. +600 on Saturday); the extra is taken evenly from the other days of that week, never below a safe floor; streaks judge each day against its adjusted goal. Not "save as you go" (would reward under-eating, against D24) |
| Festival / shaadi days | **Skip day, capped**: a marked celebration day neither adds to nor breaks the food streak (like a workout rest day); up to **4 per month**; mark it on the day or up to 2 days after |

## Fasting: research (2026-09-25)## Fasting: research (2026-09-25)
- **No single vrat rule set** ("varies region to region, family to family", Kalra et al., IJEM 2015, PMC4319259). Common North/West ground: no wheat, rice, maida, sooji, besan, dal, onion, garlic; sendha namak; kuttu, singhara, rajgira, samak, sabudana, tubers, fruit, dairy, nuts, makhana allowed. Ekadashi (Vaishnava): no grains or legumes at all. Nirjala = no water. Tamil Navaratri eats sundal (pulses); Bengal's Durga Puja is a feast (fish, mutton). Contested: samak, turmeric, hing, mustard, garam masala, tomato, peanuts, salt, phalahar-only. Jains avoid the root vegetables that are Hindu vrat staples. Food lists for single vrats come only from cultural/recipe sites: no peer-reviewed list.
- **Implication:** tag foods by **ingredients** (INDB `recipes.xlsx` has them): `grain`, `legume`, `onion_garlic`, `table_salt`, `contested_spice`, `root_veg`; offer presets (Navratri North, Ekadashi strict, Phalahar, Jain, Custom) + per-food "allowed / not for me"; always say "your family's rules may differ".
- **Sourced vrat foods already in the catalog:** sabudana khichdi (INDB OSR099), makhana kheer ASC283, aloo halwa ASC297, sookhe aloo ASC178, dahi aloo BFP242, lauki kheer BFP334, kaddu halwa ASC298, raitas (BFP318, ASC276, ASC272), peanuts IFCT H012, paneer L003, milk L001/L002, fruit, coconut water K002, dahi USDA 171284.
- **INDB recipes to add:** sabudana-peanut vada ASC379 (has garam masala), apple sabudana kheer OSR013, rajgira ladoo OSR021, sookhi arbi BFP263 (onion + garlic: not vrat), masala arbi BFP264, peanut raita ASC270, banana raita BFP326, paneer kheer ASC287, coconut kheer OSR012, makhana korma ASC225, kamal gatta halwa OSR020, fruit salad ASC265 (cornflour), peanut chikki ASC382, suran fried BFP275. **Not vrat as written:** sabudana vada BFP425 (maida), buckwheat pancake OSR106 (maida).
- **Raw:** IFCT rajgira A001/A002, samai (little millet) A016, arbi F004, sweet potato F013/F014, tapioca F015, water chestnut F016 (check species vs *Trapa*), suran F017, lotus root F005, dates E017–E019, khoa L004, coconut H006/H007; USDA buckwheat flour 170687, tapioca pearl 169717, amaranth grain 170682, lotus seeds 170149 (≈ makhana). **No source found:** singhara atta, kuttu roti/puri, samak (barnyard millet), sama kheer, plain sabudana kheer, roasted makhana: need a manufacturer label.
- **Calendar:** no reliable open API for India (Nager.Date has no India; data.gov.in nothing). Official DoPT holiday list is a yearly PDF and has no Ekadashi/Navratri; Ekadashi dates differ by tradition. → **Users mark days/ranges** (optionally repeating); DoPT list only as typed-in suggestions; day types neutral across faiths (fast, festival, Ramadan, Lent, Paryushan).
- **Intermittent fasting:** Cochrane 2026 (CD015610.pub2): "little to no difference" vs standard diet advice; BMJ 2025 NMA: similar to calorie restriction. Not advised: pregnancy/breastfeeding, under 18, underweight, eating-disorder history; diabetes on insulin/sulfonylureas needs a doctor (hypoglycaemia). Kuttu atta food poisoning happens most Navratris (Delhi, 23 Sep 2025): prefer packaged, in-date flour.
- **Safety copy** (before IF mode or a nirjala day): "Fasting isn't for everyone. Please check with your doctor first if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, under 18, underweight, have diabetes (especially on insulin or sulfonylurea tablets), have kidney or heart disease, or have ever had an eating disorder. Break your fast if you feel dizzy, shaky or confused. On nirjala fasts, drink enough water before sunrise and after the fast. Research shows IF works about as well as regular calorie control, not better."
- **Streaks (keeps D24):** a fast day is excluded from the food streak, never "on target"; no fasting streaks or countdown pressure.
