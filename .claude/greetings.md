# Greetings (Today header)

Status: **complete 2026-09-25**: 1,940 reviewed lines in 7 batches (1 general; 2 early + morning; 3 afternoon + evening; 4 night + late; 5 streak, back after a break, first day; 6 at the gym, workout done, rest day; 7 nothing logged, on track, Monday, weekend). Every time of day and every moment has lines.

## What the owner asked for
A greeting line at the top of Today every time the user comes back, in an Indian voice: cool, motivating, comedic, English or Hinglish ("Yooo dude, ready to build the body?"). A hardcoded list, generated by browser Claude from a prompt.

## Why this shape (reviewed with the owner)
- **Hardcoded, not a live LLM call:** free, instant at first paint, works offline and for guests, and the tone is reviewed once. A weekly personalised batch could come later.
- **~600 good lines, not thousands:** an LLM writing 1000+ lines repeats itself; at 2–3 views a day, 600 lines with an 80-line no-repeat window lasts months.
- **Context beats random:** the line fits the moment when the app knows one (streak, back after a break, at the gym, rest day…). That's what keeps it from going stale.

## As built
| Path | Role |
|---|---|
| `data/greetings/*.json` | Batches as returned by browser Claude (`text, time, ctx, tone, lang, bro`; optional `topic` / `work` override the auto tags). Drop new batch files here (any name; reviewed ones are renamed `batch-N.json`) |
| `scripts/build-greetings.mjs` | `npm run greetings`: validates every rule (enums, ≤ 60 chars, `{name}`/`{streak}` only, `{streak}` only in ctx streak, no emoji/Devanagari, no duplicates across files), tags `topic`, writes the catalog. Fails without writing on any error |
| `src/data/greetings.generated.json` | BUILT: `{id, t, time, ctx, topic, bro, work}`; `id` = hash of the text, so "recently shown" survives rebuilds |
| `src/lib/greet.ts` | PURE picker: `timeSlot`, `moments`, `fits`, `pickGreeting`, `greetParts` |
| `src/components/today/Greeting.tsx` | Reads the store, picks, remembers, animates. Pool loaded on demand (dynamic import) |

**Picking** (`pickGreeting`):
1. Filter what fits: time slot (late < 4, early < 7, morning < 12, afternoon < 16, evening < 20, night), no `bro` lines for a female profile, no `{name}` lines without a name (guests), no `work` lines on Saturday/Sunday, no gym lines for food-only users, no gym lines on a rest day you're resting except the rest-day lines themselves ("kal milte hain gym mein"), no general / Monday / weekend gym lines (mostly "go train" nudges) once today's workout is logged unless you're still at the gym, and no any-time `nothing_logged` lines ("log kiya ya bhool gaye?") before 1 pm.
2. Each moment that applies gets a chance to win (first_day 100 %, back_after_break 90 %, at_gym 85 %, rest_day 60 %, workout_done / streak 50 %, nothing_logged / on_track / monday 40 %, weekend 30 %) if it has unseen lines.
3. Else a general line; 40 % of the time one for this time of day if any exist.
4. The last 80 shown are skipped; if everything was seen, an older general line repeats.

**Moments** (from the store, on the device): streak = the Today flame streak ≥ 3; first_day = nothing logged before today; back_after_break = last log ≥ 3 days ago; at_gym = active visit; workout_done = a workout today; rest_day = chosen rest day (gym users only); nothing_logged = from 8 am, no food today (mornings: breakfast nudges only); on_track = food logged today and not over the goal until 8 pm (the streak's 80–105 % rarely holds before dinner), the real 80–105 % from 8 pm (D24: a night "report card A plus" never goes to a day of under-eating); monday; weekend.

**Rotation:** `localStorage["prana-greet"] = {id, at, recent[]}`. The same line stays until the app has been away 30 min (`at` is stamped when the app is hidden); tapping the line picks another. Picks wait until auth has loaded, so a saved `{name}` line survives a reload.

**UI:** full-width line above the Today layout (phone: up to 2 lines, desktop: 1), display font, words rise in one by one (reduced motion: fade only), `{name}`/`{streak}` in the turmeric→saffron gradient. Space is reserved so nothing jumps.

**Work tagging:** lines mentioning office, meeting, standup, laptop, commute, inbox, metro, traffic, college, class, canteen, desk, tuition, school bus, WFH, tiffin/lunch box are weekday-only. Batch 1's "Motivation is out of office" sets `work: false` (idiom).

**Topic tagging:** `gym` if the line mentions lifting (set, rep, squat, dumbbell, leg day, workout, gains…), else `food` if it mentions food, else `general`. A batch line can set `topic` itself; batch 1 overrides 6 ("Baaki sab set" isn't a gym set, "Form first" is).

## Adding a batch
1. Paste the prompt below into browser Claude and ask for the batch you want (all 7 are done; new lines for any time/moment are welcome). Ask for about half English and more filmy lines: batches 2–7 came out ~85 % Hinglish, ~4 % filmy.
2. Save each reply as `data/greetings/batch-N.json`.
3. `npm run greetings` (fix anything it reports), then read the lines once for tone: the script can't judge food guilt or a bad joke.

## The prompt (for browser Claude)

```
You are writing greeting lines for "Prana", a calorie + gym tracker app made for Indians (tagline: "Discipline, one katori at a time"). Every time the user opens the app, ONE short line appears at the top of the home screen. The vibe: a hype desi gym buddy who's also funny. Think a friend from college who lifts, roasts you lovingly, and quotes Bollywood.

## Voice
- Mix of Indian English and Hinglish (Hindi in ROMAN script, never Devanagari). Roughly half "hinglish", half "english" (Indian English with desi slang is still "english").
- Tones: "hype" (motivating), "funny" (comedy, light roast), "chill" (relaxed, warm), "filmy" (a twist on a famous Bollywood dialogue or desi pop-culture moment; twist it, don't quote song lyrics).
- Desi references welcome: chai, Sharma ji ka beta, mummy, rotis, dal chawal, jugaad, cricket, auto-rickshaw, monsoon, exam season, "beta", Maggi at 2 am, shaadi season, etc.
- Vary openings and sentence shapes. Don't start more than 5% of lines with the same word ("Yo", "Chalo", "Aaj"…). No two lines with the same joke.

## Hard rules (break one = line rejected)
1. Length: 60 characters max (count them), ideally 25–50. One sentence, maybe two short ones.
2. Celebrate habits, never food restriction: no guilt about eating ("samosa khaya? shame"), no "skip a meal", no starving, fasting-for-weight or "burn off that dessert" ideas. Food is fuel, not a sin.
3. No body shaming or appearance jokes (mota, fat, belly, skinny, etc.).
4. No abusive/crude slang (no gaali, even mild), no sexual jokes, no religion, caste, politics, region/state stereotypes, or jokes about any group.
5. No numbers about calories, weights, or health claims ("lose 5 kg", "500 kcal").
6. No emojis, no hashtags.
7. Placeholders: {name} = the user's first name. Use it in about 15% of lines, only where it reads naturally. {streak} = number of days in a row (only in "streak" lines; write it so it works for 3 or 300). No other placeholders.

  "tone": "funny",         // hype | funny | chill | filmy
  "lang": "hinglish",      // hinglish | english

}
```
